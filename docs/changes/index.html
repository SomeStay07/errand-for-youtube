What changed in Errand

Changes you'll notice, version by version. When a YouTube change breaks something in Errand, the fix goes into the Breakage log.

0.3.0

  • The home question shows up with the page's first frame: YouTube's grey loading boxes no longer flash before it.
  • The "Here for" label at the bottom of the page has a magnifier and is now a link back to the search results for your errand. Close tab in the end-of-video prompt also clears the errand, so your next visit starts with the question again.
  • Mixes and playlists play on. When YouTube shows the list next to the video, Errand doesn't stop the next one or ask after each video.
  • With Hide recommendations on (it is, unless you picked "Just hide Shorts"), search results no longer show recommendation shelves like "People also watched". Untick it to get them back.
    • The "Latest from" shelf of a channel is hidden too; the channel card and its videos in the results stay.
  • Shorts are hidden in more places: as ordinary-looking cards next to and under videos, and in a YouTube language that doesn't say "Shorts" (like Japanese), where the Shorts tab and search chip may flash briefly first. Playables, YouTube's games, are hidden along with Shorts.
  • YouTube's new video page layout, with recommendations under the player, is covered: they're hidden there too, and Show comments moves keyboard focus to the comments. Links that start with /live/ work like normal video pages.
  • The "!" now waits for a second failed check, so a slow page raises it less often. A real problem shows in about 12 s. When nothing is wrong, the menu says "No problems found on this page".
  • Strict mode hours that cross midnight now follow the day they start on: with Friday ticked, 22:00-06:00 runs from Friday night into Saturday morning. Picking "On a schedule" no longer locks the hours before you set them.
  • The toolbar tooltip updates when a schedule window or a pause ends.
  • The end-of-video prompt closes with Escape and returns focus to the player. Screen readers announce the 10-second countdown once instead of every second.
  • The hint under the home question and on the welcome page no longer sends every browser to the puzzle icon: browsers Errand can't recognize get a general hint.
  • After an update like this one, the menu shows "What changed" for up to 3 opens. After 5 finished errands it asks for a store rating, at most 3 times; × ends it.
  • Clearer wording in the menu, the welcome page and the home question in all five languages.
  • Smaller fixes:
    • After an update, YouTube tabs that were already open no longer keep stray buttons from the old version.
    • The Show recommendations button can't slip in while strict mode is turning on.
    • YouTube's button for hiding end cards no longer floats alone over the last seconds of a video.
    • Settings changes can't be lost while strict mode ends.
    • A cleared time field keeps the saved time.
    • Mixes stay visible on your playlists and library pages.

0.2.0

  • A new first screen after install lets you pick how YouTube should look: "Ask what I came for", "Just hide Shorts" or "Subscriptions first". You can choose again later from the menu.
  • "Pause 15 min" in the menu turns Errand off for a quarter of an hour after a 10-second wait, then it comes back on by itself.
  • Strict mode is harder to switch off on a whim: turning it off takes a 10-second wait, and on a schedule it stays off only until the current hours end. Settings can't be imported while strict mode is on.
  • The menu shows how many errands you finished in the last 7 days.
  • Subscriptions no longer show the "Most relevant" shelf when the feed is hidden.
  • Fixed a false "!" on the icon on normal video pages. The "!" now clears by itself once the page looks right again.
  • The menu links to a public breakage log.
  • The countdown before recommendations appear no longer asks "Are you sure?".
