How to use YouTube without recommendations

Pause watch history and YouTube stops filling the home page with recommendations, but video pages still show them. To remove the column next to every video, the end screen and autoplay, use an extension.

Built in: pause watch history

Open youtube.com/feed/history and pause watch history. With history off, the home page no longer shows a recommended feed. The cost: you lose your history, and recommendations stay next to every video and at the end of it.

Built in: the autoplay switch

The switch at the bottom of the player turns autoplay off. YouTube remembers it per browser, and the end-of-video suggestions still appear.

With an extension

Errand removes the recommendation column on video pages, the end screen and end cards, Mixes and the "Next" button, and keeps autoplay off outside playlists. More on the end of a video: how to hide the YouTube end screen. The home page asks "What are you here for?" instead of showing a feed. If you need recommendations, "Show recommendations" brings them back after 10 seconds. Strict mode removes that button during the hours you pick.

Questions

Will YouTube still learn from what I watch?

Yes. An extension only changes what the page shows you; YouTube's own history and recommendations work as before unless you pause watch history.

Can I still see recommendations sometimes?

Yes. In Errand, "Show recommendations" on a video page reveals the column after a 10-second wait.

Does it hide comments too?

By default comments sit behind a "Show comments" button. You can turn that off in the menu.
