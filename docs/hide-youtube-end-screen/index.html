How to hide the YouTube end screen

Turning autoplay off stops the next video from starting, but the end screen, its tiles and the wall of suggested videos still show up. To hide them you need a content filter or an extension.

What shows up at the end of a video

  • The end screen: tiles with videos, playlists or a subscribe button that the creator puts over the last 5 to 20 seconds. YouTube calls these tiles end screen elements; most people say end cards.
  • The video wall: a grid of suggested videos that covers the player once the video stops.
  • In fullscreen, the "More videos" grid with suggestions that slides up over the picture.

What YouTube lets you do

The autoplay switch at the bottom of the player stops the next video. It doesn't touch the end screen or the video wall. Creators decide whether a video has an end screen at all, so on some videos you won't see one.

Option 1: a uBlock Origin filter

In uBlock Origin, open the dashboard, go to My filters and paste:

www.youtube.com##.ytp-endscreen-content
www.youtube.com##.ytp-ce-element
www.youtube.com##.ytp-videowall-still
www.youtube.com##.ytp-modern-videowall-still
www.youtube.com##.ytp-suggestion-set
www.youtube.com##.ytp-fullscreen-grid

These rules match YouTube's player as of November 2026. When YouTube renames these parts of the player, the filter stops working without telling you.

Option 2: an extension

Errand hides the end screen, end cards, the video wall and the fullscreen "More videos" grid, and keeps autoplay off outside playlists. When your video ends, it asks "Got what you came for?" and offers to close the tab. If YouTube changes the player and Errand can't find the end screen any more, its icon shows "!".

If you picked "Just hide Shorts" on install, the end screen stays as YouTube shows it. Turn on "Hide end-of-video suggestions" in Errand's menu to hide it.

Questions

Does turning off autoplay hide the end screen?

No. It only stops the next video from starting. The end screen tiles and the video wall still appear.

Why do some videos have no end screen?

Creators add end screens themselves, and not every video has one.

Do playlists still play with the end screen hidden?

Yes. Errand hides the suggestions, and a playlist moves on to its next video as usual.
