How to hide YouTube Shorts on desktop

Short answer: YouTube has no setting that turns Shorts off on a computer. "Not interested" hides a Shorts shelf for a while, then it comes back. To remove Shorts for good you need a content filter or an extension.

What YouTube lets you do

  • On the home page, the Shorts shelf has a menu with "Not interested". YouTube hides that shelf for a while; it returns later.
  • Pausing watch history empties the home page, Shorts shelf included. You lose the whole home feed, and Shorts still appear in search and on channel pages.
  • There is no setting for Shorts in search results, the subscriptions feed or the sidebar.

Option 1: a uBlock Origin filter

If you already use uBlock Origin, open its dashboard, go to My filters and paste:

www.youtube.com##ytd-rich-shelf-renderer[is-shorts]
www.youtube.com##ytd-reel-shelf-renderer
www.youtube.com##ytd-rich-item-renderer:has(a[href^="/shorts/"])
www.youtube.com##grid-shelf-view-model:has(a[href^="/shorts/"])
www.youtube.com##ytd-guide-entry-renderer:has(a[href^="/shorts"])
www.youtube.com##ytd-mini-guide-entry-renderer:has(a[href^="/shorts"])

These rules match YouTube's layout as of November 2026. When YouTube renames these page elements, filters stop working without telling you, and you have to update them yourself.

Option 2: an extension

Errand hides Shorts on the home page, in search, in subscriptions, on channel pages and in the sidebar. Links to Shorts open as normal videos with regular controls, or can be blocked completely. After each page load it checks whether any Shorts slipped through and shows "!" on its icon if YouTube changed something.

If Shorts are all you want gone, pick "Just hide Shorts" when you install. The feed, recommendations, comments and the end screen stay the way YouTube shows them.

Add to Chrome

Other extensions that hide Shorts: Unhook, UnTrap, Remove YouTube Suggestions, Control Panel for YouTube. See the comparison. The end of a video has its own guide: how to hide the YouTube end screen.

Where Shorts show up

  • the shelf on the home page
  • the "Shorts" tab on channel pages
  • the left sidebar
  • links shared from phones, which open the vertical Shorts player

Shorts in search results

Search mixes a Shorts grid in with the videos and adds a "Shorts" filter chip above them. YouTube has no setting for either. Errand hides the grid, single Shorts in the results and the chip.

Shorts in the subscriptions feed

The subscriptions feed shows a Shorts shelf from the channels you follow, and YouTube has no setting to turn it off. Errand hides that shelf and single Shorts in the feed.

Questions

Can I watch a Short as a normal video?

Yes. In the address, replace /shorts/ with /watch?v= and keep the video ID. Errand does this automatically for every Shorts link.

Does hiding Shorts change what YouTube recommends?

Not directly. Hiding only stops Shorts from being shown to you; YouTube's recommendations are unchanged.

Does this work in the YouTube app on my phone?

No. Browser extensions work in desktop browsers; the YouTube app on phones can't be changed by extensions.
